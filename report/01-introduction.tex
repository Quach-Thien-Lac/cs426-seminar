Secure coding often presents itself as a challenge through exploits and vulnerabilities. Theese risks can range from being benign (wrong text layout) to rendering the program potentially harmful to its users and developers (leaked passwords, data tampering). As such, defensive programming techniques and security best practices are often greatly emphasized during development.

To this end, we highlight the monumental importance in mitigating software vulnerabilities via our demonstration --- Sanguosuo, a board game (Sanguosha) wiki. We choose three vulnerabilities most prevalent in modern softwares: denial-of-service attacks (DoS), SQL injections (SQLi), and data breaches.
