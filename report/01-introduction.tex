Sanguosha (\begin{otherlanguage}{vietnamese}Tam Quốc Sát\end{otherlanguage}) is a strategy card and board game based on the historical Three Kingdoms period of ancient China. Players choose heroes drawn from that era, each with unique skills, and compete across different roles in turn-based play. The game has a loyal international fanbase, an extensive roster of heroes and cards, and a rich set of mechanics that players frequently reference during sessions.

Sanguosuo (\begin{otherlanguage}{vietnamese}Tam Quốc Sách\end{otherlanguage}) is a mobile wiki application built for the CS426 Mobile Device Application Development seminar. The name combines \emph{Sanguosha} with \emph{s\'ach} (Vietnamese for ``book'' or ``encyclopedia''). Its purpose is to give players a convenient reference on their Android devices, reducing the need to search across scattered wikis or printed rulebooks during play. Users can look up heroes by name or identifier, view their faction, health, epithet, skills, and skill tags, and browse hero--hero synergy combinations.
